Proficiency levels

A
Awareness — recognizes the topic, can describe it in plain language, knows who owns it. Does not perform the work. ~1 hr to reach.
W
Working — can perform the task with a reference doc or light guidance. Not yet trusted unsupervised on a live customer. ~4 hrs.
P
Proficient — performs independently on live customer work, including common edge cases. Needs no supervision. ~1 wk.
E
Expert — designs the approach, resolves novel cases, sets the standard, trains others. Owns the outcome. ~1 mo.
—
Not required — out of scope for this role. Route it rather than attempt it.

Time estimates are cumulative from a standing start with mentor access and a sandbox tenant, ±50% — for planning, not for holding anyone to a date.
